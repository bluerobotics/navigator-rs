21//! RPPAL provides access to the Raspberry Pi's GPIO, I2C, PWM, SPI and UART
22//! peripherals through a user-friendly interface. In addition to peripheral
23//! access, RPPAL also offers support for USB to serial adapters.
24//!
25//! The library can be used in conjunction with a variety of platform-agnostic
26//! drivers through its `embedded-hal` trait implementations. Both `embedded-hal`
27//! v0.2.6 and v1.0.0-alpha.5 are supported.
28//!
29//! RPPAL requires Raspberry Pi OS or any similar, recent, Linux distribution.
30//! Both `gnu` and `musl` libc targets are supported. RPPAL is compatible with the
31//! Raspberry Pi A, A+, B, B+, 2B, 3A+, 3B, 3B+, 4B, CM, CM 3, CM 3+, CM 4, 400,
32//! Zero, Zero W and Zero 2 W. Backwards compatibility for minor revisions isn't
33//! guaranteed until v1.0.0.
